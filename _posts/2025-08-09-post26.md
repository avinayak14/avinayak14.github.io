<br/>
It had been a while since I was in Northern Virginia (five days), so it was nice to head to the DMV for the weekend for NS+AM's wedding, staying at A Aunty's place. After Top Gun on the flight over, I reached at night, picked up the rental car, and headed to McLean.
<br/>
<br/>
Friday had no daytime events, so I worked from home in the morning, then went to a café in Bethesda called maman for meetings. In the evening came the Sangeet at Rose Hill Manor, with a potpourri of delicious dishes from Silk Banquet in Leesburg (which catered some friends' wedding earlier in 2025). Paneer tacos, aam palak chaat (round two), and a cocktail bar, followed by a night of revelry, dancing, and conversation. Great to meet so many SF folks from my time hanging with Nick in the Bay, plus plenty from NY and DC. Nick's groomsmen remind me of my Princeton homies — intelligence and career prowess paralleled with complete boolery. A really entertaining group.
<br/>
<br/>
Saturday upped the ante despite Friday's fun. The baraat opened the morning with Nick on a horse — TOASTY. A delicious array of North Indian breakfast delicacies with lemonade and badam lassi, then a wholesome ceremony and a full Punjabi-mode lunch. N+A had a magazine that's one of the cutest meet-cute representations I've seen, from their first Hinge convo screenshot to wedding photos. After a few hours' sleep, we returned for the reception: Raj Kachoris, aloo tikki, and everything in between at entry. Each guest had personalized notes on placards at the center of the hall at White's Ferry (déjà vu) — sweet, and it reminded me of the notes KKT wrote at his wedding last year. After the surprise Attan dance we'd practiced for A, more dance-floor vibing, and dessert, I left around 11pm for an early morning.
<br/>
<br/>
Sunday, thanks to the RKD-maestro, I visited my 40th state! RD took a 4:30am train from Philly to DC at 6:30am, and we drove to Harper's Ferry, West Virginia, to hike and explore the small town. The lad has a wonderful partner, and even through married life and crushing it at DeepMind, his social energy stays tall as ever. The maestro and I had a maelstrom getting to the park with his sheer inability to navigate, causing me to redirect several times; since he'd just finished Peter Attia's Outlive, we joked my driving might be the fifth Horseman.
<br/>
<br/>
An amazing weekend with a lot of solid people. Till the next adventure.
