<br/>
On Friday 06/07 I woke early for a 7am flight to Denver. Flights carry far more turbulence than in years past, presumably from the rapidly warming troposphere. We landed on time around 10:45am, and I took an Uber to Novo Coffee downtown to work for the day.
<br/>
<br/>
In the late afternoon D/B/KKT picked me up, and we grabbed Torchy's (my first ever!). The fried avocado tacos were delicious, the poblano salsa and chipotle aioli great complements. Then to the Airbnb to check in and play foosball and Pacman in the rec room. After picking up P near Union Station, we got food from Aloy Modern Thai. The rest of the crew arrived around 10pm. We'd booked a Rocky Mountain National Park timed entry for 6–8am but pushed it to the afternoon to sleep in a bit.
<br/>
<br/>
The next morning, after a makeshift breakfast of pb/belvita bars/bananas, we headed to the Flatirons Loop in Boulder for our first hike — a 2.7-mile trail, of which we did about two miles roundtrip, prioritizing lunch. We found Parma Trattoria & Mozzarella Bar, sharing a Quattro Stagioni pizza, a breaded eggplant pizza, and a hearty bucatini cacio e pepe with truffle oil. Food comas set in as we drove toward Rocky Mountain National Park, the rain and hail picking up. We stopped at Party City for a sash and tiara for K and "I Do Crew" pins for everyone. The tiara was locked tight with plastic belts, but J broke it open with a key and a safety pin (who needs scissors?). On the way we stopped at nearly a dozen vista points for photos of views, moose, and elk. Our elevation swung between 10K and 12K feet until we reached the Continental Divide at Milner Pass, where the watershed splits between the Atlantic and Pacific. In snow packed several feet deep, we exited our cars in the shorts and t-shirts we'd confidently worn for 85-degree hikes, and nearly all of us braved the 40-degree walk to the top of Milner. We were rewarded with incredible views of snow-capped peaks and cloud formations.
<br/>
<br/>
After that chilly adventure, we switched drivers and headed to Adelitas Cocina y Cantina, recommended by J — some of the best Mexican food I've had in the U.S., on par with Quinto Sol in Redwood City. Their Naranja Fresca cocktail blends mezcal (Madre Mezcal Espadin, batch-made in Oaxaca), orange bell pepper, sour orange, and cantaloupe; the mezcal's smoke complemented the cantaloupe's sweetness. Back at the Airbnb, I showed the guys some of my favorite comedians — Ronny Chieng and Ramy Youssef — before we hit the sack after midnight.
<br/>
<br/>
In the morning we made breakfast and hung out, spending time on the roof deck (whose grill we never got to use). We split into two cars by flight time; after dropping the rental (filling gas within five miles of return, per the rule), we shuttled to the airport and said our goodbyes. The flight back was turbulent leaving amid a thunderstorm but mellowed out, and I watched "The Beekeeper" with Jason Statham.
<br/>
<br/>
A wonderful trip! Thanks to everyone for making it a blast, to R for planning the whole thing, and to KKT for being a great sport while his lads made endless fun of him. Though the population skewed UTA boys, I was glad to be an ancillary member.
