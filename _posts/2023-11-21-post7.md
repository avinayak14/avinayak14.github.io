<br/>
It had been several years since I'd last visited India, but a ripe occasion arose with the wedding of a close family member. Earlier in 2023, once the dates were finalized, I booked tickets through Lufthansa.
<br/>
<br/>
On the morning of Nov 2, I did some last-minute packing and headed to the airport right after lunch for my flight to Frankfurt. My first international trip in five years seemed like it'd need more luggage, but I ended up with two carry-ons (a backpack and a small suitcase) and one check-in, mostly empty. As a light packer, I'm continuing the trend.
<br/>
<br/>
After an avocado mozzarella sandwich and a herb tonic juice from Joe & the Juice, I made my way to the gate. Two strangers beside me struck up conversation with interesting stories: one had taught ballet in Germany since 1980, and the other was a 72-year-old Redwood City native flying to watch a Kansas City Chiefs game with his daughter and son-in-law. I usually keep to myself on flights, but we spent three hours on everything from Airbnb to operettas to ageism. I slept most of the rest of the way and read a few research papers on recommender systems.
<br/>
<br/>
Reaching Frankfurt, I decided it's the worst airport I've been to: loud crowds, slow and disorganized baggage checks, poor bathrooms, delays, and ostensibly ubiquitous rudeness from staff. I don't plan to fly through again. Luckily I ran into some aunties and uncles at the gate of my next flight, and we commiserated over the service. I slept through most of that flight and reached Bangalore a few hours late, around 3am on Nov 4.
<br/>
<br/>
After a couple hours of sleep at my relatives' place, K and K arrived in the morning and we ate breakfast before heading to V's. It was great to see V and T/K in person after four years, since San Diego in 2019. The newest addition to the family, N, is adorable, and we had tons of fun with her over the week. We mostly chilled Nov 4 and the day of Nov 5 to recover from jetlag. That evening, K and I went with V to a restaurant/bar in Bengaluru Urban to meet some of V's friends coming from Pittsburgh — there may or may not be an implicit height requirement. They were super cool, and I got a Cosmopolitan and a Whiskey Sour, my first bar drinks in India.
<br/>
<br/>
On the 6th, a group of us went to Bangalore Cafe, where I had what I believe is the best restaurant dosa I've ever eaten — two dosas coalesced into one, crispy outside and soft inside. I'm not usually a coffee person, but I had several filter coffees on this trip, including one there. The rest of the day was relaxing and helping with logistics. On the 7th I met family for thalis at Woodlands. In the afternoon K and I went with V to pick up alcohol for the necessary pre and post games, and in the evening K^2/T/V/N and I went to Namma Adigas for some of the best food I've had in India (not exaggerating): gobi chilli fry, kaju masala with cheese garlic naan, and chaas.
<br/>
<br/>
We woke early on the 8th and drove an hour to the wedding venue at Ambari. After transporting luggage and setting up, the day's events began with several religious/traditional ceremonies. In the evening came the sangeet, where I sang one of V's favorite Arctic Monkeys songs and danced in a group number to Naatu Naatu we'd rehearsed. V and A did couple dances too — a wonderful time!
<br/>
<br/>
The next day was the culmination of the wedding events, where I met more relatives before we headed home in the evening. Friday was a chill day. Saturday brought two fun outings: visiting a music teacher I used to learn from, and my great-uncle and his family. Sunday was Diwali, spent with close relatives, with a small group heading to Kailash Parbat for excellent chaat, North Indian sabzis, and dessert (a falooda and a brownie with ice cream).
<br/>
<br/>
Flying back early Monday morning, I had low expectations after Frankfurt. But the several-hour layover in Munich couldn't have been better: despite being sleep-deprived, I used an airport shower lounge (without flying business/first), grabbed food, and dealt with very pleasant staff. Munich now has my full confidence — I'll fly through there next time if I can.
<br/>
<br/>
Overall, a great time spent with cousins and in-laws! Unmentioned: we spent 95% of our free time in Bangalore watching the ICC World Cup on TV.
