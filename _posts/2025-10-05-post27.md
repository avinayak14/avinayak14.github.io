<br/>
It's funny how adulthood can let the most fortuitous friendships burgeon, some through arbitrary connections and some through years of shared experiences. R and I went to college together but only had our first extended conversation in early 2024, when S (a friend I met through my Princeton RCA friend D) invited me to TopGolf. We found a lot in common, and I've visited him at Google for lunch around 30 times in the past year and a half. R is one of the two funniest friends I have, and also an incredible community builder, creative artist, and human — someone I turn to for advice on the ethical questions behind my decisions. One of the luckiest friendships of my adulthood.
<br/>
<br/>
R and A married in the DMV this fall, and it was a delightful time. After finishing work on 10/3, I flew SFO to Dulles direct and stayed the night in Arlington. The next day had two ceremonies — one at 1:30pm and one around 5:30/6pm. The first was a Nikkah at the Diyanet Center of America in Lanham, where I luckily ran into my guy ME-D. The mosque is architecturally beautiful, run by a Turkish-government-funded nonprofit founded in 1993. The Nikkah was smaller than the evening reception but wholesome and concise — mutual consent, a marriage contract (Nikah-Nama), and witness signatories. Afterward many of us congregated outside to catch up, and D+S kindly gave me a ride back toward Arlington (most others, like P and H, stayed in DC proper).
<br/>
<br/>
After a 45-minute power nap, I headed to the Smithsonian Postal Museum for the reception — a beautiful amalgam of Afghan, Palestinian, and Lebanese culture. Starting with hors d'oeuvres, the speeches began with S crushing his. PQN and I hung out at our table, and a cool touch was that the whole Postal Museum was open for viewing; we walked parts of the National Philatelic Collection, including the famous "Inverted Jenny" error stamp and John Lennon's childhood stamp collection. After a delicious Afghan dinner (catered by Sheeryakh in Alexandria) and knafeh and fereni, we vibed on the dancefloor with the #bayboys, and I left around 10pm. The next morning PQN and I went food-and-museum hopping: bento boxes at Teaism Dupont, then the Smithsonian Museum of Natural History and the Air & Space Museum. MAN is it helpful to walk a museum with PQN — any question, and he has a thoughtful answer.
<br/>
<br/>
After catching my flight back that afternoon, I was coming down with a slight cold and called it an early night. An amazing trip to celebrate two very special people.
