Anyone who knows me knows Breaking Bad ranks among my favorite pieces of art ever created. The ability to recite certain episodes from the last three seasons may or may not be in my arsenal.
<br/>
<br/>
I had to visit the famed house (now on the market for $4M+), so I made a quick trip to ABQ for less than 36 hours. On the 28th I met up with my friend N and nerded out heavily at the National Museum of Nuclear Science and History. Then the Breaking Bad house, what is likely the only Vietnamese restaurant in ABQ (the banh mi was delicious), Mufasa in theaters, and a fennel sausage pizza at Farina Pizzeria and Wine Bar downtown. I'd never live in Albuquerque (and likely won't visit again), but I had to pay respects to Bryan Cranston and Aaron Paul. That said, I could be convinced to hop over to Santa Fe and Los Alamos for a weekend.
