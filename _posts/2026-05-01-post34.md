<br/>
A quick weekend in Seattle with S/A, K, and W friends, built around great food, high-quality coffee, and a thorough walk through Pike Place.
<br/>
<br/>
I flew in on Friday 5/1 and landed around 6:30pm, then went straight to dinner with S/A at Araya Thai, which is a completely vegan restaurant. We shared fresh lettuce rolls with peanut sauce, wide rice noodles in a sweet-savory sauce, and a basil noodle dish with tofu, broccoli, and zucchini, plus a cast-iron pot of tea that made the table feel very official. Dessert was at Snowy Village and was decadent: a tower of purple shaved snow topped with a scoop of ice cream, red bean, and sliced almonds, which we demolished much faster than expected.
<br/>
<br/>
I stayed at S/A's place at Centerline,  where the lobby resembles a boutique hotel, with wood paneling, hanging globe lights, and an unreasonable number of plants. The gym was excellent as well :)
<br/>
<br/>
Saturday started with a walk through Green Lake Park and along the lake, under trees just coming into leaf and bright blue skies that Seattle starts to promise more truthfully as May rolls around each year. After a latte with S, my W buddies and I met up for brunch at Portage Bay Cafe: somehow, we managed to snag a table in the sunny window where nobody was in a hurry. SF moves in 2.5x in comparison.
<br/>
<br/>
That afternoon I met up with my friend K from Microsoft who owns Offline Coffee Co., a Chinese-inspired coffee & matcha cafe with tasty baked goods. She had started the cafe after working for 2 years in BigTech, and she runs it incredibly efficiently; they were brimming with eager customers, and particularly a plethora of college students and young professionals. It was warm enough that iced coffee was pretty much the only reasonable order, and I stuck around long enough to watch half the neighborhood come through for the same thing.
<br/>
<br/>
Then came Pike Place with S & A. We walked past a chai-inspired neon sign, peered into a cheesemaker's window, and lost a good 8 or so minutes at The Miniature Car Dealer, a tiny shop lined with model cars and a Route 66 sign, with a Space Needle in the corner. In need of food, we stopped by Riccio's Sausage Co, which had a variety of sausages and hot sandwiches with a sign proudly listing its best sellers. Subsequently, at Mick's Peppourri we sampled pepper jellies (three or more jars gets you a dollar off each, which is how a person ends up with three jars). After salivating long enough, we proceeded outside to consume our sandwiches on a bench in the warm sun.
<br/>
<br/>
We ended the afternoon at the market's rooftop overlook, with the downtown skyline sharp against a very blue sky, and after grabbing dinner in the evening in Capitol Hill, I caught my morning flight on Sunday with no issues. After 9 years since my last visit, it was fortunate that Seattle took a raincheck on greeting my arrival with precipitation; a great weekend with great people!
