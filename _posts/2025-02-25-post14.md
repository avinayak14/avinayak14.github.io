<br/>
After a mini belated birthday celebration on Dec 24 with A/R/N, cat-sitting for A & J on Christmas Day, and a lunch-and-movie with V in Daly City, I headed to SFO on Boxing Day to ring in the new year with K, S, and A in Cleveland. The flight to Phoenix was bumpy, but Phoenix to Cleveland was smooth; I mostly slept and finished "The Return of Great Powers" by Jim Sciutto.
<br/>
<br/>
Little did we know that the afternoon of 12/27 at BOSS Pro-Karting would kick off an ungodly five-day Mario Kart frenzy. My win rate flipped between real go-karting and Mario Kart. After three heats, we grabbed chaat and dosas at Bombay Chaat and spent a restful evening at home.
<br/>
<br/>
A and I slept past noon almost every day, so the 28th was mostly chilling at home before sushi at Sakana in Beachcliff Market Square (some of the best we'd had). Our OpenTable reservation hadn't registered, so we killed time with outdoor pistol squats, a massage parlor, and a video game store. On the 29th, A, K, and I tried a golf simulator, then stopped by Target for extra Switch controllers and Carcassonne before dinner at Luxe Kitchen & Lounge, a delicious Mediterranean spot in the Detroit Shoreway area. After a couple of Carcassonne games, we hit the sack.
<br/>
<br/>
On the 30th we started promptly around 12:30pm, packed up in the afternoon, and headed out in the evening after S finished work. Stopping at Piada in Dublin, OH for dinner, we reached our Airbnb before midnight. We all learned on New Year's Eve that Indianapolis, despite a population of nearly 900K, could have a bit more going for it. After a tour of the Indianapolis Motor Speedway and the culture-rich Eiteljorg Museum (fantastic — A and I helped finish a hard jigsaw puzzle before leaving), we'd seen nearly everything we wanted. After chai in Fountain Square, we had a delicious NYE dinner at Bluebeard downtown, then capped the night watching the NYC ball drop and playing more Mario Kart.
<br/>
<br/>
After driving back to Ohio through snow on the 1st, we dropped A at the airport and had a quiet night. The 2nd was a chilled-out day with even more sleep, ending at a quirky bar with snakes-and-ladders games paired with questions on 2024 experiences and the half-decade lookback.
<br/>
<br/>
On the 3rd I flew back via Charlotte, using the four-hour layover to knock off my 30th state with a quick trip to Fort Mill, South Carolina and its history museum. All in all, an amazing way to close out the year with the sibling squad — and an honor to win the household's "most hours slept" award by what is likely a factor of two.
