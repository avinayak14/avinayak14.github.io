As long as I've known family members my age, I've had immense admiration for T — someone (along with my cousins and bro K) I can treat as a peer while learning loads from. It was incredibly special to celebrate his bachelor party in NYC over the summer-solstice weekend.
<br/>
<br/>
Flying through Chicago Midway, I was set to reach Thursday June 19 to stay with G+J before the weekend at the Airbnb with all the guys. But after reaching Chicago, all flights to NYC were cancelled for the evening due to severe thunderstorms. After calling K a few times to sort out options (and seeing no reasonable way to Cleveland for the night), I stayed an extra night in Chicago and flew out the next evening. It worked out nicely — I got work done from Chicago Friday, and caught a late lunch with SK and AA at Machamantales before Midway. The flight was reliable the second time, but traffic from LGA to the Airbnb was abysmal (at 9:30pm on a Friday), and I arrived around 10pm. After pong at the homestead, we bar-hopped at Bird in Hand and the Vale Public, wrapping up around 12:30am to save energy for the big day (Saturday is for the boys).
<br/>
<br/>
Saturday started excellently with brunch at one of the best Thai spots I've been to in the U.S.: Fish Cheeks in the LES. S's fiancée and her fam had spoken highly of it, and it delivered — coconut crab curry, crispy pork, ground beef, and spicy tofu. After adding to our step count café/bar-hopping (Dark Matter Coffee, Mr. Purple's rooftop, The Long Pour, COPS, and Blind Tiger), we took a brief Airbnb respite before dinner at Malecon, another top-tier spot. Then we pre-gamed at the house, each giving 30 seconds of praise to T in a circle before trash-talking at rage cage and more — great to observe as the guy who drinks the least. In two cars, we hit a rave at the Mirage in Brooklyn (Middle Eastern and Latin house). We left in an early and a later group; I left with the early group at 3:45am, having a flight the next morning.
<br/>
<br/>
An incredible weekend. Excited to run it back at the wedding in August!
