<br/>
A quick weekend in Seattle with SNA and K, built around good food, better coffee, and a very long walk through Pike Place.
<br/>
<br/>
I flew in on Friday 5/1 and landed around 6:30pm, then went straight to dinner with SNA at Araya Thai. We shared fresh lettuce rolls with peanut sauce, wide rice noodles in a sweet-savory sauce, and a basil noodle dish with tofu, broccoli, and zucchini, plus a cast-iron pot of tea that made the table feel very official. Dessert was at a Korean snow cone spot nearby: a tower of purple shaved snow topped with a scoop of ice cream, red bean, and sliced almonds, which we demolished faster than I'd like to admit.
<br/>
<br/>
I stayed at SNA's place at [COMPLEX NAME], where the lobby looks like a boutique hotel, with wood paneling, hanging globe lights, and an unreasonable number of plants. [GYM LINE]
<br/>
<br/>
Saturday started with a walk through a park and along the lake, under trees just coming into leaf and bright blue skies that Seattle does not usually promise. After a latte, we had brunch at Portage Bay Cafe: I got a giant folded tortilla with guacamole and greens, and we snagged a table in the sunny window where nobody was in a hurry.
<br/>
<br/>
That afternoon we met K at Offline Coffee Co., a café with a cloud logo and a sidewalk full of tiny stools. It was warm enough that iced coffee was the only reasonable order, and we stayed long enough to watch half the neighborhood come through for the same thing.
<br/>
<br/>
Then came Pike Place with SNA. We walked past Aditi Chai's neon sign, peered into a cheesemaker's window, and lost a good ten minutes at The Miniature Car Dealer, a tiny shop lined with model cars and a Route 66 sign, with a Space Needle in the corner. An Italian deli had a case of sausages and hot sandwiches with a sign proudly listing its best sellers. At Mick's Peppourri we sampled pepper jellies (three or more jars gets you a dollar off each, which is how a person ends up with three jars). We ate our sandwiches, mozzarella and basil on one and something saucy on the other, on a bench in the sun.
<br/>
<br/>
We ended the afternoon at the market's rooftop overlook, with the downtown skyline sharp against a very blue sky. It was the best photo of the weekend, and I have the sunglasses selfie of the three of us to prove it.
<br/>
<br/>
My flight out on Sunday was early, so it was a quick goodbye. A great weekend with great people, and I'll take another one whenever SNA will have me.
