I made a short trip to NYC last week — my first in three years. It's a wonderful city (I lived there for three months in 2017), and here are a few words about the visit.
<br/>
<br/>
After finishing the workday, I headed back to San Jose from Mountain View. Leaving the office around 5pm, I expected some 101 traffic, but made it home in under 20 minutes. I did laundry so both of my adidas 3-stripe joggers could be packed — one each for the eastbound and westbound flights. The ride to SJC was 10 minutes, and there I had to check in my carry-on.
<br/>
<br/>
JetBlue's Blue Basic fare (the United Basic Economy corollary) allows only a small personal item; a carry-on costs $35 to check. Sneak one to the gate and it's $65 at boarding. A bit extra, frankly, but befitting the deal I got on the roundtrip, so I checked my small red suitcase. PSA for the Boston trip in June and any fellow travelers.
<br/>
<br/>
The red-eye left around 10:30pm PDT and reached NYC around 7am EDT. Blessed with a middle seat, I couldn't doze for the first two hours because the guy to my right was humming in his sleep — remarkably on pitch, decent-sounding, but annoying on a flight where sleep is limited to a 5h30m window. After several rounds of solitaire and battleship (the only two in-flight games), I fell asleep.
<br/>
<br/>
Turbulence woke me about an hour out; the Fitbit had tracked 2h37m of sleep, with nothing more to tack on. At JFK the AirTrain was shut down for the weekend, so the MTA ran a free shuttle to Howard Beach. On the train, a woman sat down beside me and started speaking Spanish (don't ask how or why). She was looking for a friend's place in New Jersey — her first time in the U.S., fresh off a civil engineering program in Colombia (the country, not the college). She was job-hunting in New York and learning English. We had a great conversation, and since we were headed the same way (I was going to my relatives' place in Jersey City), I helped her sort out directions to Newark Penn. We rode together to the WTC stop, then caught the PATH toward Newark (I got off earlier at Grove St). It's been a while since I've had that spontaneous an interaction. She had a 50-pound suitcase, and after I offered to (struggle profusely to) haul it up and down maybe 10 flights of stairs at WTC, she kindly offered to pay for my train tickets. Good cardio and good vibes, but I was winded for five minutes after.
<br/>
<br/>
Before noon I reached my relatives' place — a couple with two of the most adorable kids (almost 6 and 2). We biked along the waterfront in Newport and hit Smorgasborg for food. I rented a Citibike, honestly a 6/10: the ride's good, but a new 2022 municipal rule makes every $15 day-pass holder dock the bike at an automated stand every 30 minutes or get charged. My aunt's sister was once hit with $100 over three hours, so I stayed diligent.
<br/>
<br/>
That evening I met a friend at Toloache, a solid Mexican spot in Midtown near Trump Tower — wild to finally see the infamous escalator in person. After all my Mission District Mexican food, I was skeptical, but it was great. Tasty sangrias too, though I could barely finish mine (I don't drink much).
<br/>
<br/>
Sunday was more of the same with the relatives and their kids. The five-year-old is quick-witted and hilarious: told to share a toy with his brother for five minutes, he grabbed my uncle's phone and set a timer. Watching them juggle two kids and demanding jobs, I was impressed — hope to reach their level of parenting one day. Then a massive Trader Joe's haul in Hoboken, and I cooked two pastas: a tri-color fusilli à la vodka ("red creamy pasta") for the kids, and a pesto linguini with roasted mushrooms, diced onions, sun-dried tomatoes, olives, roasted red peppers, and bomba sauce for the adults. Came out pretty well.
<br/>
<br/>
Monday and Tuesday were mostly work (meetings and presentations), with Monday evening at The Uncommons, a board game café in lower Manhattan, for codenames and pizza. At night we watched The Dropout, the Hulu miniseries on Elizabeth Holmes. The acting and writing were superb, though the storytelling felt rushed, skimming the backstories of characters like Sunny and Edmond. Entertaining enough that I'd have welcomed more.
<br/>
<br/>
Before heading out, I grabbed a couple of toys for the kids at Newport Shopping Center. The trip to the airport was surprisingly efficient — 1h10m door-to-door from Jersey to Terminal 5. I read on the Kindle and snacked on brown sugar cookies, Cheez-Its, Pop Corners, and a cinnamon granola bar, then passed out.
<br/>
<br/>
A short but sweet trip!
