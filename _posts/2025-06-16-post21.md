<br/>
Technically this was a staycation in the woodlands of southern Los Gatos, but I'll count it as travel since I flew across the country for it the weekend after the Karvokhi-arathon.
<br/>
<br/>
Back from DC around noon, I freshened up, got some work done, then suited up for Saachi's wedding. Since I was singing for them (Aaj Kal Zindagi), I made use of some vocal rest and sleep on the six-hour flight and at home. Around 3:10 I left for Nestldown in Los Gatos, in time for the 4pm ceremony. It was refreshingly simple and concise — not that I don't dearly love friends and family who've had longer ceremonies, but the juxtaposition is appreciated :) A processional, exchange of vows and rings, and a Christian prayer paired with a Jain mantra recited by M Aunty.
<br/>
<br/>
At the happy hour, N, R, and I had nice catch-up time (the gobi peanut chili cauliflower was incredible), plus a round of bocce and a ride on the mini-train that's part of the rustic venue. At dinner, with N, R, I, and our families tabled together, I sang the Hindi song after a brief intro, and it went well! The dinner — particularly the kofta and palak sabzi — was delicious, with tiramisu, mango mousse, and ice cream falooda to round it out. S uncle's speech was absolutely hilarious, including the story of M Aunty calling J a "white boy with Indian values." N and I couldn't stop laughing.
<br/>
<br/>
After cheering J + S's first dance, N and I tore up the dance floor for an hour or two (R's not a big dancer, though we had an awesome time with him at the earlier events), then headed home — it being a Sunday — after saying our goodbyes. Overall, a concise but incredible celebration of a dear friend of 20 years, and great to spend quality time with the family.
